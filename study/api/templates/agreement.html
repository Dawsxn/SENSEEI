Reading these

Cohen's κ is what §4.6.5 specifies and what to report. It counts every disagreement equally, which on an ordered three-point scale is harsh: Developing against Proficient is a smaller gap than Beginning against Proficient, and unweighted κ cannot tell them apart. The weighted column is there to look at alongside, not instead.

"undefined" means both raters used a single category throughout, so chance agreement is already total and there is no room above it to measure. It is not perfect reliability and must not be reported as 1.0.
