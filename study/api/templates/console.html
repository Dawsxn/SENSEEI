Watch for, but do not act on

A highlighted row means a measure is sitting at zero, or an incident is logged. Zero usually means something is broken rather than that the participant is idle — an unguided participant with no turns twenty minutes in is most likely looking at a failed model connection, which is worth fixing while the session is still running.

It is not a reason to prompt someone to engage more. Judging engagement mid-run and nudging accordingly would be an intervention, and an unblinded one. Fix broken tools; leave participants alone.
