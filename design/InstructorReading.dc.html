Porter's Five Forces

How much profit an industry earns is not an accident. Porter argued that five forces decide how much of the value an industry creates its firms get to keep. Rivalry among existing firms is only one of them.

Figure 1. The five forces

The strongest force sets the ceiling on profits, and it is the one strategy has to answer first. In airlines, powerful buyers and fierce rivalry keep margins thin even when demand is high.
