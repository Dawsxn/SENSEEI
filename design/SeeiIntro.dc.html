How SEE-I works

In SENSEE-I, you learn a concept from a reading by going through the four steps of SEE-I:

State
Say what the concept is, briefly and clearly.
Elaborate
Explain it in more detail, in your own words.
Exemplify
Give your own example of it, plus one thing that is not an example.
Illustrate
Compare it to something outside the reading, like an analogy or metaphor, that is easy to picture.

Core components and the rubric

Components

These are the key ideas of the reading, which you should keep in mind as the session revolves around them. Some criteria from the rubric also reference them.

Rubric

The rubric holds the criteria that each of your answers is graded on. Each SEE-I step has its own, which describes the traits of an expected answer for that specific step.

Example
BrevityThe statement is concise and straight to the point. It does not contain any unnecessary filler, repetition, or tangents.

When an answer fails a certain criterion, its name shows as a red tag under the tutor's reply.
