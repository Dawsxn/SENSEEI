Showing what a response missed

Six ways to tell the student which criteria were not met, on the same failed State attempt. C and D need nothing from the backend: the Tutor Agent already names the criteria in its sentence, and markdown is what weights them. A, B, E and F take the names out of the prose and send them as data, which also means the tutor has nothing left to bold.

Always visible, nothing to open. Reads as the outcome of the attempt it sits under.
